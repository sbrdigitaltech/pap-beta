ABOUT

Company overview

Background

PAP is an aspiring property management consultancy firm with a combined experience of over 100 years in mall management, specifically operations, leasing and marketing.

We deliver mall management solutions that help new malls and high streets succeed from day one through smart strategy and execution, and revive struggling malls into profitable assets.

Mission

To redefine the future of retail real estate through intelligent planning, seamless execution, and result-driven strategies.

Our approach

Four phases, in sequence, with no handover gaps.

Need gap analysis

Check brand potential of the local populace and understand floating traffic to build the right leasing plan.

Pre-launch preps

Ensure technical, BMS, security and operations are set up correctly to avoid recurring issues post launch. SOPs, manuals, leasing and ROI calculations.

Launch phase

PR plans, a 360-degree marketing plan, launch event, and implementation of SOPs for ops, security, housekeeping, BMS and technical systems.

Sustenance

Ongoing mall operations, marketing and churn across leasing, maintenance, AMCs, security, housekeeping upgrades and marketing calendars.

Impact of our approach

A project driven by data, catchment insight and strategic planning leads to high footfall, a strong brand mix and early profitability. Every aspect, from leasing to launch, stays aligned with market demand.
