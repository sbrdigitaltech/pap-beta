Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device, typically so it can remember your actions or preferences. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels. Together we refer to these as “cookies” in this policy.

2. Cookies on this website

papindia.in does not set advertising or analytics cookies. We do not track you across other websites, and we do not build advertising profiles from your visit.

  • Strictly necessary behaviour: the website stores nothing on your device for its own operation: menus, forms and page behaviour work without cookies.
  • Third-party requests: the website loads fonts from Google Fonts and submits the contact form to our database provider. These providers may receive your IP address and device information as part of the request, in line with their own policies.

3. If that changes

If we later add cookies, for example privacy-friendly analytics, we will update this policy, list each cookie with its purpose and lifespan, and, where required, ask for your consent before setting anything non-essential.

4. Managing cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Every major browser lets you view, delete and block cookies per website. Blocking cookies does not affect your ability to browse this website.
